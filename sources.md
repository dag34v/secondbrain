# Sources

Master appendix for `second-brain.html`. Inline markers on each slide resolve here.

## Slide 1 - Why now

1. Affinity, *Private capital predictions for 2026*. Survey of ~300 dealmakers
   finds 85% use AI to automate daily tasks, up from 76% a year earlier.
   <https://www.affinity.co/report/affinity-predictions-report>
2. Affinity, *Unlocking the power of AI in venture capital*. AI is now used
   across sourcing, diligence, monitoring, and LP reporting.
   <https://www.affinity.co/blog/ai-in-venture-capital>
3. Affinity, *10 AI Tools for Venture Capital Firms in 2026*. The current
   tool landscape targets funds, not studios.
   <https://www.affinity.co/guides/vc-ai-tools>
4. Author observation. Searching public catalogs (Affinity guide, Standard
   Metrics tech stack list, G2 categories) returns zero "venture studio
   second brain" products. Verified by inspection, not by a published count.

## Slide 3 - The flywheel is the moat

1. Global Startup Studio Network via Bundl. 72% of studio companies reach
   Series A vs 42% of traditional. Reach Series A in 25.2 months vs 56.
   <https://www.bundl.com/articles/why-venture-studio-startups-have-higher-long-term-success-rates>
2. Mandalore Partners, *How Venture Studios Leverage Intangible Assets*.
   Codified playbooks, networks, and institutional knowledge are the
   studio's durable advantage.
   <https://www.mandalorepartners.com/research/comment-les-venture-studios-valorisent-les-actifs-immatriels-de-leurs-startups>
3. Esinli Capital, *Venture Studio vs VC Fund*. Studios typically take
   30-60% equity, vs 10-20% for a lead VC. Studios embed teams.
   <https://esinli.com/knowledge-base/startup-models/venture-studio-model/>
4. High Alpha, *What is a Venture Studio*. Studios run ~5 builds at a time
   with the same team and reusable infrastructure.
   <https://www.highalpha.com/resources/venture-studio>

## Slide 5 - The co-pilot dial

1. Atlan, *Autonomous Agents vs Copilots*. Risk is the defining difference;
   only 21% of enterprises have mature governance for autonomous agents.
   <https://atlan.com/know/autonomous-agents-vs-copilots/>
2. arXiv 2510.15739, *AURA: An Agent Autonomy Risk Assessment Framework*.
   Liability and risk scale with delegated authority and reversibility.
   <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.15739>
3. Foley & Lardner, *Agentic AI Liability in Autonomous Decisions* (2026).
   Responsibility chain across user, configurator, and vendor becomes
   unclear as autonomy increases.
   <https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/05/agentic-ai-liability-in-autonomous-supply-chain-decisions-identifying-and-preventing-legal-risks/>
4. Domo, *AI Copilots vs AI Agents*. Copilots let teams observe AI
   capability before granting autonomy. Trust-build pattern.
   <https://www.domo.com/blog/ai-copilots-vs-ai-agents-understanding-the-difference-and-choosing-the-right-approach>

## Slide 9 - What would make this fail

1. MeltingSpot, *Digital Transformation Failure Rate 2025*. ~70% of
   transformations fail to meet objectives; Gartner reports only 48% of
   projects fully meet targets.
   <https://blog.meltingspot.io/why-digital-transformation-projects-fail/>
2. Sci-Tech Today, *ERP Software Statistics 2025*. Inadequate change
   management contributes to 42%+ of failures; 55% over budget, 68% over
   schedule.
   <https://www.sci-tech-today.com/stats/enterprise-resource-planning-erp-software-statistics/>
3. Integrate.io, *Data Integration Adoption Rates 2026*. 63% cite
   underutilised apps as a driver of SaaS consolidation; 25-30% of IT
   spend wasted on unused tools.
   <https://www.integrate.io/blog/data-integration-adoption-rates-enterprises/>
4. Marlborough Street Partners, *Don't Let Your Software Turn into
   Shelfware*. Adoption is mostly a change-management problem, not a
   software problem.
   <https://www.marlboroughst.com/think/dont-let-your-software-turn-into-shelfware>
