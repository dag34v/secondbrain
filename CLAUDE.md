# 34V Second Brain - Deck Conventions

This file governs how the partner deck (`second-brain.html`) and any
supporting artifacts are written and edited.

## Writing rules

- Short sentences. Active voice.
- No jargon. If a term needs a gloss, write it inline.
- Use simple dashes (`-`). Never em-dashes (`—`).
- Numbers cited as written in the source. Round only when the source rounds.
- If a figure cannot be verified, say so on the slide. Do not invent.

## Slide structure

Every slide leads with its conclusion, then evidence, then implication.
Each slide carries four blocks, in this order:

1. **CLAIM** - the position the slide is defending.
2. **EVIDENCE** - cited facts that support the claim.
3. **WHY IT BEATS THE ALTERNATIVE** - the comparison that justifies it.
4. **WHAT 34V SHOULD DO** - the implication for the studio.

## Citations

- Every external fact gets an inline marker (`[n]`) on the slide.
- Speaker notes for each slide list the full sources behind that slide.
- `sources.md` is the master appendix. Every `[n]` resolves there.

## Tone

- Audience is 34V partners. Internal decision session, not a pitch.
- Opinionated. The deck takes positions. It does not survey.
- No filler ("in today's world", "in the age of AI"). No hype.
